energy of 0.659, valence of 0.484 and danceability of 0.716, the listener's aura can be described as a mixture of

While these averages do provide a snapshopt of the listener's aura, they may not be an accurate representative of
listener's personality completely. The dataset lacks data like, the amount of times a track has been listened to, the
time of the day, or listening purpose. The categories based on which the listener's aura is being judged is also very broad
as you can't really judge a person's whole personality based on the averages of just 3 categories while there is much more
to a music.
A rather more accurate way of judging someone's aura would be to also consider the amount of times a person played a certain
song, when did he play it, did he go back to the song after a while or was it jsut part of the trend. Also taking into account
the person's listening habits from more than one year would help capture the evolving preferences and result in a more
robust audio aura.
